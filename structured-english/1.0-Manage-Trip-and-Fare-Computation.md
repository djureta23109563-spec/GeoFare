#1.0 Manage Trip & Fare Computation

```text
BEGIN

    GET Passenger_Pickup_Location
    GET Passenger_Dropoff_Location

    CAPTURE GPS_Coordinates for Pickup_Location
    CAPTURE GPS_Coordinates for Dropoff_Location

    CALCULATE Trip_Distance using GPS_Coordinates

    GET Official_Base_Fare from Fare_Rate_DB
    GET Per_Kilometer_Rate from Fare_Rate_DB

    CALCULATE Estimated_Fare using Trip_Distance,
        Official_Base_Fare, and Per_Kilometer_Rate

    DISPLAY Trip_Distance and Estimated_Fare to Passenger

    STORE Trip_Details and Estimated_Fare in Trip_Logs

END
